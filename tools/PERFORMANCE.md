## What's working / what isn't
- Baseline (v1 slideshow reels): 76–89% of viewers swipe away almost immediately; the average viewer watches 2–5 s of a ~50 s reel. The first second is the whole problem.
- The cricket topic (002) held viewers about twice as long as UPI (003): 5.1 s vs 2.3 s, with a lower skip rate. Early hint that famous people and sport beat abstract economy numbers. Too few posts to call it a pattern.
- Zero shares and saves so far. Endings now rotate a "send this to..." prompt.
- From post 005, reels use the v2 builder (hook-first cover, motion, captions, 25–35 s). Compare v2 skip rate and avg watch against this baseline.
