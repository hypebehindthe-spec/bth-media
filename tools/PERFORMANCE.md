## What's working / what isn't (14 Nov, 9 reels, owner-requested pull)
- Totals: Instagram 1,087 views (reach 940), median 120 per reel, every reel 88–143. Instagram shows each reel to a ~100-person cold test batch and stops there: skip rates of 70–89% and almost no shares mean no reel has earned a second push yet. Engagement across all 9: 3 likes, 1 share, 1 save, 0 comments.
- p010 IRCTC is the first real break: 47.7% skip and 10.7 s average watch after only 8 h (page average otherwise ~76% and ~5 s). Hook: a brand everyone uses + a surprising culprit ("Blame the water"). Watch whether Instagram pushes it past ~140 views.
- YouTube: 1,141 views, median 98 per Short, and growing with every post: p007 PVR 336, p006 Tata Steel 254, p009 Tata Power 185, p005 Sivakasi 137 vs 12–29 for the first three. Recognisable company names in the title pull YouTube search and feed traffic. Likes 3, comments 0.
- Illustrated reels (p006–p010) average 71% skip vs 79% for the four older reels; the A/B test leans illustrated.
- Benchmark: ZeroInOn (Bangalore history, new account, 14 Nov screenshots) gets ~2.4–2.75K Instagram views per reel and 1.2K per Short, plus a 7-minute long video (1.4K views, 35 subscribers). Differences: one story with a person and a twist told as a long video plus Shorts cut from it, cinematic 3D scenes, hyper-local landmarks, a persistent title bar, cross-linked bio. See PLAYBOOK "Story reels".

## What's working / what isn't (12 Nov, 7 reels)
- Instagram reach is still flat: 88–142 views per reel (median 120), all cold audiences. p008 got the page's first share and the most views (142) in only 13 h.
- Illustrated reels skip less: 70.9–73.6% (avg 72.1%) vs 74.5–89.2% (avg 79.5%) for the four older reels, and hold longer (avg 5.7 s vs 4.5 s). The best hold on the page is p007 (7.4 s, 70.9% skip). p007 and p008 aren't 48 h old yet, so the A/B verdict waits for Monday's pull.
- YouTube is where the page grows: the three newest reels (249, 325, 60 in 13 h) and p005 (133) beat the first three (12–30) by 5–20x. Results decodes and guess-the-number reels with company names in the title are pulling search and feed traffic.
- Guess-the-number + X vs Y (p007, hook score 70) is the best on every measure except Instagram views; the highest hook score so far also has the lowest skip rate. Three data points; keep logging.
- Abstract economy numbers (003 UPI) remain the worst on every measure.
- Caveat on the A/B test: the "last 3 v2 reels" don't exist yet (only p005 is v2; 002–004 are v1), so the comparison is illustrated vs everything older. Reels from p009 go back to v2 and give a cleaner baseline.

## Audience (Instagram, posts 002 and 004, 8 Nov)
- 100% of views from non-followers; 84–86% from the Reels tab, 14–15% from Explore. Instagram is testing every reel on cold audiences, so the first second decides everything.
- Men 94–99%; ages 18–34 make up 85–90% (25–34 slightly ahead on cricket, 18–24 on gold); India 84–88%, small NRI share (US, UK, Canada, Australia).
- Write for young Indian men: cricket, phones and gadgets, money in your pocket, cars and bikes, jobs and salaries, startups and famous brands. Keep language simple and direct.

## Posting time
- 002 and 003 went live at about 1–2 AM IST (approved late at night); 004 at 2:38 PM IST and got the best watch time and lowest skip rate of the three. Not proof on its own, but posting into dead hours wastes the first test audience.
- From 8 Nov, Approve queues a post for 6:47 PM IST (12:47 PM too when there is a backlog or the topic is about today). "Post now" stays available for urgent posts.

## YouTube Shorts
- 23 views in the first 2 days; 74% from the Shorts feed, 13% from YouTube search, 13% from channel pages. Search already brings views, so titles keep their keywords.
- The UPI Short held viewers for 55% of its length on YouTube versus 2 s on Instagram: same video, very different first-second behaviour. Don't judge a topic on Instagram alone.
