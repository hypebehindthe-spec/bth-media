You'd need cement and steel to stop a river. A Chola king used only stones. Two thousand years later, it still works. How?

This is Kallanai, on the Kaveri near Trichy. Built by King Karikala Chola, around the second century.

329 metres लंबा, और एक भी पत्थर तराशा नहीं गया।

The trick? Huge uncut boulders, sunk straight into the sandy riverbed, in running water.

It splits the Kaveri into four streams, and fed sixty-nine thousand acres of farms.

Centuries later, British engineer Arthur Cotton studied it, and learned how to build on sand.

सोचो, जो सिखाने आए थे, वो यहाँ से सीखकर गए।

By the 1900s, it watered about ten lakh acres. India's oldest dam still in use.
