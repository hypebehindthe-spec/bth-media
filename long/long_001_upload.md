## Description (copy everything between the lines)
---
In 1969, Karsanbhai Patel, a lab assistant in a Gujarat government department, started making detergent in a 10x10 ft room at home and sold it door to door on a bicycle for ₹3.50 a kilo, when Surf cost ₹13. By the mid-1980s Nirma was India's best-selling detergent, and Hindustan Lever answered with a strategy called STING and a new brand, Wheel. This is the story of Nirma, the real girl on the packet, and how a detergent brand became a soda ash, cement and pharma group.
