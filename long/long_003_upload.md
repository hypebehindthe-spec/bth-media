In 2010, Kochi Tuskers Kerala paid $333 million (about ₹1,533 crore) for an IPL team. It played 14 matches in 2011, and then the BCCI terminated it. Fourteen years later, the Bombay High Court upheld awards of about ₹538 crore against the BCCI. This is the full story: the bid, the tweet that cost a minister his job, the one season, the bank guarantee, and the 15-year legal fight.
